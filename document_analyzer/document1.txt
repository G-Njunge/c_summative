The Internet of Things, or IoT, connects everyday objects to the internet.
A sensor in a field can measure the soil and send the result to a phone.
Farmers use IoT sensors to decide when to water their crops.

Homes use it too. A smart lamp is a small iot device.
Every device needs power, a sensor and a way to send data.
IoT security matters, because an IoT device with a weak password is easy to attack.
